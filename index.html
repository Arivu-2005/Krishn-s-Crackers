Celebrate Responsibly: Diwali Safety Tips

Ensure a joyful, hazard-free festival for your family, children, and pets

👕

Wear Cotton Clothes

Always wear well-fitting cotton attire and secure footwear. Avoid loose synthetic garments near fire.

🪣

Keep Water Buckets Handy

Keep two buckets of water and sand nearby for emergency extinguishing and disposing burnt sparkler wires.

📏

Maintain Safe Distance

Always light aerial cakes and rockets in wide open spaces and step back at least 15 meters immediately.

🐕

Pet Safety & Low Sound

Protect pets by keeping them inside comfortable soundproofed rooms. Choose our certified Eco-Green low decibel fireworks.
